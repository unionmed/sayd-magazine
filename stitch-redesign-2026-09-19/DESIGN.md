## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, high-luxury Arabic digital journal celebrating nature, falconry, equestrian traditions, and wildlife stewardship. The visual aesthetic fuses traditional Arab heritage with contemporary continental editorial refinement—evoking the prestige of archival natural history folios framed through sharp modern digital interfaces.

### Core Character
- **Majestic & Noble:** Rooted in falconry, desert horsemanship, and heritage expeditions.
- **Editorial Rigor:** Content-first layouts, pristine typographic hierarchies, deliberate line measures, and dignified micro-details.
- **Ecological Mastery:** Natural, deep-rooted colorations evoking juniper highlands, cypress thickets, desert dunes, and burnished saddle leather.

### Design Movement
**Refined Contemporary Editorial (Modern Classic):** The aesthetic pairs crisp structural rhythm, subtle warm parchment surfaces, razor-thin hairline brass rules, and rich typographic contrast with full RTL-first layout considerations.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the habitats of the Levant and Arabian Peninsula: deep highland pine and cedar forests (`#133326`), radiant antique gold (`#C59B27`), raw saddlery earth tones (`#8C6239`), and warm desert parchment (`#FDFCF7`).

### Color Roles & Guidelines
- **Primary (`#133326`):** Anchors headers, major display headlines, category banners, mastheads, and primary action surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#C59B27`):** Reserved for prestige editorial flourishes—exclusive tags, awards, gold foil divider rules, active category indicators, and breaking news labels.
- **Tertiary (`#8C6239`):** Accents author bylines, sub-department metadata, image captions, and warm divider borders.
- **Neutral (`#191D1A`):** A tinted forest-charcoal replacing pure pitch black for comfortable long-form Arabic body reading without harsh ocular strain.
- **Parchment Surfaces (`#FDFCF7`, `#F6F3EA`):** Gentle, low-glare reading canvases reminiscent of unbleached archival paper.
- **Accent Crimson (`#B82E2E`):** Specifically deployed for live broadcast alerts, urgent conservation bulletins, and breaking tickers.

## Typography

The design system employs **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** across all tiers to achieve rigorous typographic discipline. Its structured humanist mechanics yield flawless legibility for intricate Arabic ligatures, retaining poise across high-density news columns and sprawling long-form prose.

### Typographic Directives
- **RTL Baseline Alignment:** Generous vertical line-heights (`1.6x` to `1.7x` on body text) account for ascenders and descenders characteristic of Arabic letterforms (e.g., Alif, Lam, Jim, and Yeh).
- **Heading Character:** Display titles utilize heavy weights (`700`) paired with subtle warm dark tones to establish definitive hierarchy without decorative visual noise.
- **Editorial Sub-details:** Dates, locations, photo credits, and issue folios use `label-sm` with tight precision and clear RTL numeral formatting.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column editorial grid underpins all pages, providing dynamic tension between monumental feature imagery and dense multi-column news indexes.

### Grid & Density
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, and `2.5rem` page margins. Max layout bounding box capped at `1380px` for optimal reading comfort.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8 columns, `1rem` gutters, and `1.5rem` margins. Sidebars collapse to anchored sub-grids below featured stories.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `0.75rem` gutters, and `1rem` safe margins with seamless full-bleed imagery integration for editorial impact.
- **RTL Fluidity:** Spatial flows start naturally from right to left; metadata chips, category tags, and timestamps align systematically to the right margin.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids generic digital drop-shadows in favor of tactile editorial layering, warm tonal framing, and brass hairline borders.

### Stratification Model
1. **Base Ground (`#FDFCF7`):** The unadorned parchment canvas upon which all articles sit.
2. **Subtle Surface (`#F6F3EA`):** Enclosed modules, breaking tickers, issue archives, and side panels, separated by `1px` borders in `#E8DCB8`.
3. **Elevated Cover Cards:** Clean cards rendered with a hairline boundary (`rgba(19, 51, 38, 0.08)`) and a diffused warm ambient shade (`0 8px 24px -6px rgba(19, 32, 22, 0.07)`).
4. **Hero Overlays:** Darkened gradient scrims (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(12, 35, 26, 0) 35%, rgba(12, 35, 26, 0.92) 100%)`) over high-resolution wildlife and expedition photography, maintaining stark legibility for white headline typography.

## Shapes

The design system implements a controlled **Soft (`1`)** shape vocabulary. Radii are kept crisp and restrained (`4px` to `8px`) to evoke the precision of classical letterpress print and cut-paper magazine binding.

- **Cards & Media Panels:** `4px` corner radius (`rounded-sm`), preventing photographic frames from appearing informal or toy-like.
- **Interactive Badges & Category Tags:** Soft rectangular cuts with a maximum radius of `3px` or pure square corners.
- **Inputs & Buttons:** `4px` uniform corner cuts, preserving geometric discipline across Arabic form fields.

## Components

### Masthead & Navigation
- **Top Utility Bar:** Deep forest green background (`#0C231A`) featuring Gregorian and Hijri calendar dates, edition toggles, and social channels.
- **Primary Journal Masthead:** Centered, dignified Arabic calligraphy lockup with hairline brass dividers (`#C59B27`) separating sections (Falconry, Equestrian, Marine Life, Conservation, Gear & Heritage).

### Breaking News Ticker (شريط الأخبار)
- Right-anchored solid crimson badge (`#B82E2E`) labelled "عاجل" or "أحدث التقارير" in bold white.
- Continuous horizontal scroll on `#F6F3EA` background flanked by a brass left-border accent.

### Editorial Hero & Lead Story
- Asymmetrical 8:4 layout. Lead side features full-bleed photography with overlaid white `display-xl` typography, golden category tag, and author metadata.
- Right rail contains stacked secondary news pieces bordered by delicate `1px` parchment-toned dividers.

### Category Chips & Badges
- **Heritage Badge:** Gold background (`#C59B27`) with deep forest text (`#133326`) and uppercase tracking for editorial departments.
- **Standard Tag:** Crisp parchment background (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px` border of `#C59B27` and deep green text.

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Action:** Solid forest green fill (`#133326`) with gold hover border transition (`#C59B27`), crisp white text, and `4px` radius.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background framed with a `1.5px` border in `#8C6239` and text in `#133326`.

### Cards & Story Grid
- **Photojournalism Card:** Image container maintaining a strict 16:10 ratio, sharp corner radius, overlaid category pill on top-right, followed by high-contrast `headline-sm` title and 2-line truncated summary.
- **Archival Text Card:** Dedicated to opinion, historical essays, and treatises; uses light parchment background (`#F6F3EA`), top brass accent rule (`2px solid #C59B27`), and prominent `title-md` typography.
