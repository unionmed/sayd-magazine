Hunting firearms at the Riyadh exhibition: broader participation and Beretta’s 500th anniversary editions

3 October 2026Sayd
Share this story

More than 5,000 products in the firearms, ammunition and accessories area, as the number of hunting and sport shooting firearms companies rises from eight to eleven.

The firearms, ammunition and accessories area at the Saudi International Falcons and Hunting Exhibition 2026 brings together more than 5,000 products, including hunting and sport shooting firearms and airguns, alongside ammunition, maintenance and cleaning tools, secure storage equipment, protection and training supplies.

Organised by the National Center for Falcons, the exhibition runs from 1 to 10 October at the Riyadh Exhibition and Convention Center in Malham, north of Riyadh. The firearms area hosts 11 hunting and sport shooting firearms companies, up from eight last year, an increase of 37.5%, alongside four airgun companies and nine suppliers of equipment, tools and accessories. More than 181 brands are represented across these categories.

This year’s exhibition coincides with five centuries since Italian manufacturer Beretta began making firearms in 1526. Its participation, through Saudi agent Al Rumaih, includes limited editions marking the anniversary, alongside hunting firearms and accessories, as well as commemorative watches and clothing. A celebration is scheduled for the exhibition’s final day, 10 October, with representatives of the Italian company attending.

Al Rumaih chief executive Ahmed Saleh Al Rumaih said the company sold a shotgun for 120,000 Saudi riyals at last year’s exhibition, its most expensive firearm sale during that edition.
