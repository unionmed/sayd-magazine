Birds of Khirbet Selm through Joumana Majed’s lens

Share this story

Four photographs of birds in Khirbet Selm, southern Lebanon, taken on 7 October 2026 by reader Joumana Majed and shared with Sayd. The images move from birds perched on wires and flying across the sky to closer views among plants.
