## Editorial acceptance tests

1. **Bird encyclopedia:** visible species diagnostic features verified, species name not inferred from filename alone.
2. **Event coverage:** photo source, location and event match independently; a historical image cannot document a new seizure or release.
3. **People:** identities verified separately; do not infer that every named person appears in the frame.
4. **Falconry and hunting:** distinguish falcon species, training, legal hunting and archive scenes; avoid misleading labels.
5. **Reuse:** check whether an image already appears in the target article, and whether it is already in ChatGPT Library; do not assume copies are synchronized.
6. **Technical:** use existing local `docs/media/` asset path, no hotlink, no image moves.
7. **Rights:** preserve source and photographer fields; permission follow-up belongs to Nayef.
