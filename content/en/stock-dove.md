Its Arabic common name is a broad everyday expression for wild pigeon, making the scientific name especially important for precision.</p><p>It lacks the Woodpigeon’s white neck patch and shows short black wing bars. Separating it from Rock Dove and domestic forms requires several characters rather than grey colour alone.</p><p>Its habitat combines farmland for feeding with large, cavity-bearing trees for nesting. BTO reports a typical clutch of two eggs. Losing old trees can therefore remove nest sites even when feeding fields remain available.</p><p>Occurrence beside farmland does not make it a feral domestic pigeon. Useful documentation combines wing and neck views with locality, without chasing the bird. The accompanying photograph is illustrative, not evidence of local breeding.</p><p>The African Bird Club documents the species in Morocco. This alone does not provide a detailed seasonal map for Arab countries or establish resident breeding wherever it is observed.</p><p class="article-sources"><a href="https://www.bto.org/learn/about-birds/birdfacts/stock-dove">www.bto.org — Stock Dove</a> · <a href="https://www.rspb.org.uk/birds-and-wildlife/stock-dove">www.rspb.org.uk — Stock Dove</a> · <a href="https://www.africanbirdclub.org/countries/morocco/morocco-introduction/">www.africanbirdclub.org — Stock Dove</a></p><p class="photo-credit">Photo: Cristina Hutchison</p>
