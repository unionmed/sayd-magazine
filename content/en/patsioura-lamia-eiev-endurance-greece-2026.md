<figure><img src="../../../media/publications/lamia-eiev-endurance-20261123/lamia-awards-cover.jpg" width="1280" height="760" alt="Award recipients and officials at the EIEV endurance awards podium in Lamia." loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto"></figure><p class="excerpt">Lamia hosted the five-stage, 143.7km feature race, with Santa Susanna in Spain announced as the next stop in December.</p><p>Greek rider Areti Patsioura, aboard Salamon Ektoras R, won the feature race at the ninth round of the Emirates International Endurance Village Global Series, held in Lamia, Greece, on Saturday, 21 November 2026.</p><p>The three-star race comprised five stages over an actual distance of 143.7km, according to the programme published by the Hellenic Equestrian Federation.</p><h2>Emirati–Greek cooperation</h2><p>The round was held through a partnership between Emirates International Endurance Village, EIEV PULSE and the Hellenic Equestrian Federation, under the supervision of the International Equestrian Federation (FEI). The event brought together international races and the Greek national endurance competitions.</p><p>The international programme included two-star and one-star races over actual distances of 121.2km and 105.6km, including competitions for young riders and juniors.</p><h2>Race titles and the awards ceremony</h2><p>Maria Roussi, riding Zagira, won one of the two-star races. Savvina Maria Theodorou, aboard Azira, won a one-star race, while Sofia Fileli, riding Hifa, claimed another one-star title.</p><p>The winners were honoured by Mahra Ali Al Shaya, Chargé d’Affaires at the UAE Embassy in Greece; Lara Sawaya, Executive Director of Marketing and Sponsorship at the village and EIEV PULSE; and the Mayor of Lamia and city representatives.</p><figure><img src="../../../media/publications/lamia-eiev-endurance-20261123/lamia-awards-group.jpg" width="1280" height="773" alt="A group photograph from the endurance racing awards ceremony in Lamia, Greece." loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto"></figure><h2>Spain is the next stop</h2><p>According to the round’s press material, the series will move to its tenth stop in Santa Susanna, Spain, scheduled for 4 December 2026.</p><p class="article-sources"><a href="https://hef.gr/index.php/en/news-en/press-release/2176-cei3-2">Hellenic Equestrian Federation — Lamia competition programme</a> · <a href="https://www.wam.ae/en/article/c3du0rl-patsioura-wins-eiev-global-endurance-series-140km">Emirates News Agency — Lamia results, 22 November 2026</a></p><p class="photo-credit">Photos: press material accompanying the Emirates International Endurance Village races.</p>
