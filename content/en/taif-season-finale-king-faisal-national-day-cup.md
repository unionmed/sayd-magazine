## Body

Saudi and Gulf racing has entered its decisive stretch. The countdown is underway to the tenth and final week of the Taif 2026 racing season, which brings the summer campaign to a close this weekend at King Khalid Equestrian Field in Al-Hawiyah.

The anticipation follows a decisive round at the end of week nine, when the Jockey Club distributed purses of nearly 2.8 million riyals and crowned five champions in the speed races and the club’s honorary contributions — heats that showed the participating stables and riders at the peak of their form.

Attention now turns to the two richest closing evenings of the summer season:

**Friday evening (25 September 2026):** the night of the major cups, led by the King Faisal Cup for purebred Arabians, classified internationally as a Group 3, alongside the Prince Abdullah Al-Faisal Cup. Large purses bring together the leading speed and middle-distance Arabians.

**Saturday evening (26 September 2026):** the season’s closing note, the National Day Cup for purebred thoroughbreds, with national and heritage celebrations around the crowning of the finale’s champion.

Al-Hawiyah is drawing a notable turnout of owners and racing followers from across the Gulf this week, for the contest over the final metres that confirms Taif as the summer capital of purebred Arabian racing and speed sports.
