<figure><img src="../../../media/publications/uae-falconry-sailing-20261106/falcon-delta-2022.jpg" width="1280" height="800" alt="A brown-feathered falcon facing right against a blurred background." loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto"><figcaption>Illustrative photograph of a falcon, taken in 2022.</figcaption></figure><p class="excerpt">Registration remains open until 12 November for the 2026/2027 season, with the league due to start in Al Ain on 3 December.</p><p>Abu Dhabi Falconers Club has opened team registration for the 2026/2027 UAE Falconers League, in cooperation with the Hamdan Bin Mohammed Heritage Center. Applications to form teams must be submitted by 12 November 2026.</p><p>Applications are submitted through the online form on the club’s website and include details of the team and its manager, together with lists of owners and falconers. Under the season’s rules, participation is restricted to UAE citizens, and team members must be at least 18 years old.</p><p>The league features four falcon categories: Jeer Taba, Jeer Shaheen, Garmousha Jeer and Pure Jeer. Teams earn points according to their falcons’ placings in the heats, which cover a distance of 400 metres.</p><p>The club’s calendar lists the league’s opening in the Al Ain region on 3 December 2026.
