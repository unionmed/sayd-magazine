<figure style="max-width:900px;margin:26px auto"><img src="../../../assets/images/2026/10/arabian-saluki-efc-asd22.png" alt="Salukis racing on a sandy track; a photograph supplied with the club’s press material." width="819" height="1024" decoding="async" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto"><figcaption>Salukis racing on a sandy track; a photograph supplied with the club’s press material.</figcaption></figure><p class="excerpt">The Emirates Falconers’ Club and Abu Dhabi’s Arabian Saluki Centre will hold the inaugural season in Al Ain and Al Dhafra from November 2026 to April 2027.</p><p>The Emirates Falconers’ Club and the Arabian Saluki Centre in Abu Dhabi will organise the first Arabian Saluki Championship in Al Ain and Al Dhafra from November 2026 to April 2027, aiming to develop this heritage sport and preserve its ties to hunting culture and desert life.</p><p>The championship brings together speed, distance and endurance races in a season built around registration, classification and cumulative points.</p><figure style="max-width:900px;margin:26px auto"><img src="../../../assets/images/2026/10/arabian-saluki-efc-asd26.jpg" alt="Handlers with Salukis in a photograph supplied with the club’s press material." width="1080" height="1350" decoding="async" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto"><figcaption>Handlers with Salukis in a photograph supplied with the club’s press material.</figcaption></figure><h2>Three distances for males and females</h2><p>Competitions comprise 1,000-metre sprints, 1,500-metre middle-distance races and 2,000-metre endurance races, with male and female categories. The season includes preliminary rounds, semi-finals and finals, with points accumulated across rounds to determine the category champions.</p><p>Five seasonal titles will be awarded: Speed Champion, Distance Champion, Endurance Champion, Arabian Saluki Champion and Best Breeder. The programme also includes cash prizes, an Arabian Saluki beauty competition, or mazayen, and a simulated hunting competition using a mechanical lure.</p><h2>A registry of bloodlines, owners and breeders</h2><p>The club says the championship will establish the first official Arabian Saluki registry in the UAE and the Gulf region, documenting bloodlines, owners and breeders and issuing annual registration and classification certificates. The system will allow each Saluki’s performance to be tracked and assessed throughout the season rather than through a single race result.</p><p>The organisers’ vision also includes a scientific database to study genetic and physical capabilities, programmes to improve bloodlines, training and assessment, and broader participation by owners, breeders and enthusiasts.</p><p>Source: Emirates Falconers’ Club.</p><p class="photo-credit"><small>Photographs: Emirates Falconers’ Club.</small></p><section class="article-more"><h2>Read also</h2><div class="reading-grid"><a class="reading-card" href="../melwah-finale-2026-60-winners/index.html"><img src="../../../media/uploads/2026/10/saudi-2026-melwah-spa.jpg" alt="Al-Melwah closes with 60 winners and a wider field" width="1280" height="853" loading="lazy" decoding="async"><span>Al-Melwah closes with 60 winners and a wider field</span></a><a class="reading-card" href="../saudi-falcons-hunting-exhibition-2026-highlights/index.html"><img src="../../../media/uploads/2026/10/saudi-2026-melwah-spa.jpg" alt="Saudi Falcons and Hunting Exhibition 2026: Al-Melwah, Auctions and Artificial Intelligence" width="1280" height="853" loading="lazy" decoding="async"><span>Saudi Falcons and Hunting Exhibition 2026: Al-Melwah, Auctions and Artificial Intelligence</span></a></div></section>
