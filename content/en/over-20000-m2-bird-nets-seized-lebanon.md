# 38,280 m² of illegal bird-trapping nets removed in northern Bekaa, Lebanon

## Body

<article class="article-content">
<p class="article-deck" style="font-size:1.12em;font-weight:600">A joint operation by the Internal Security Forces and anti-poaching teams, supported by sustainable hunters and drone surveillance.</p>

<p>The Bekaa Investigation Detachment of the Regional Gendarmerie, under the Bekaa regional command, carried out a field operation that removed 38,280 square metres of nets used for illegal and indiscriminate bird trapping. Fieldwork and intelligence were coordinated with a joint Lebanese–international team comprising the Anti-Poaching Unit (APU) of the Middle East Center for Sustainable Hunting (MECSHAP) and the German Committee Against Bird Slaughter (CABS).</p>

<p>The missions covered Younine, Labweh, Arsal, Ras Baalbek, Mashari al-Qaa, al-Kuwaitiya, Jabouleh, Jadidat al-Fakiha and the Nahleh highlands. Teams located illegal trapping sites, reached them and removed the nets. Seized material was handed over after each mission to the relevant units in accordance with established procedures.</p>
<figure style="margin:24px auto;max-width:850px"><img src="../../../media/uploads/2026/10/bekaa-nets-removal-adonis-12.jpg" alt="A field team removes illegal bird-trapping nets at an operation site in the Bekaa." loading="lazy" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:6px"><figcaption style="font-size:11px;color:#777;margin-top:8px">Source: Mecshap</figcaption></figure>
<p>The operation combined coordinated fieldwork with rapid detection and intervention. The Bekaa Investigation Detachment used a drone to survey the terrain, locate nets from the air and guide teams to the trapping sites.</p>
<p>Administrative and media coordination involved the Bekaa regional command, the Public Relations Division and the Gendarmerie Command operations room of the Internal Security Forces. Personnel from the Ras Baalbek station also helped remove some of the seized nets. Sustainable hunters in the Bekaa supplied field information identifying illegal trapping sites.</p>
<figure style="margin:24px auto;max-width:850px"><img src="../../../media/uploads/2026/10/bekaa-nets-loading-adonis-13.jpg" alt="Seized nets and poles are loaded onto a team vehicle during the field operation." loading="lazy" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:6px"><figcaption style="font-size:11px;color:#777;margin-top:8px">Source: Mecshap</figcaption></figure>
<p>A similar operation last year removed approximately 8,000 square metres of nets. According to the information supplied for this report, it was considered at the time the largest such removal in Lebanon and the largest operation of its kind in CABS’s roughly fifty-year history.</p>
<p>The current operation removed 38,280 square metres of nets over three days, highlighting the importance of cooperation among security forces, specialist teams and sustainable hunters in protecting birds and wildlife in Lebanon.</p>
