<figure><img src="../../../media/publications/libya-bani-walid-gazelles-20261123/bani-walid-gazelles.jpg" width="1368" height="911" alt="Gazelles in front of participants in the photograph accompanying LANA’s Bani Walid release report." loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto"></figure><p class="excerpt">Fieldwork and community participation support gazelles’ return to the wild, following earlier releases in the city in May.</p><p>Al-Hayat wildlife protection association announced the release of a fifth batch of 20 gazelles in valleys around Bani Walid, according to a Libyan News Agency (LANA) report published on 22 November 2026. The initiative aims to support their return to natural habitats.</p><p>LANA said fieldwork involved travelling through rugged gullies and valleys. The association intends to continue its wildlife protection efforts with volunteers and supporters.</p><h2>Earlier initiatives in Bani Walid</h2><p>The city has seen earlier initiatives. On 17 May 2026, LANA reported that the local branch of the Libyan wildlife protection association released 28 gazelles. Its report described a community pact to protect gazelles, Barbary sheep and houbara bustards from hunting, linking the initiative to tackling the effects of poaching.</p><p>On 19 November, LANA published Al-Hayat’s announcement of a gazelle release event in the Tareeq al-Nahr–Jabiyat al-Nabaq area. Residents, hunters and nature enthusiasts were invited, highlighting the community’s role in protection.</p><h2>Protection continues after release</h2><p>IUCN guidelines on conservation translocations identify suitable habitat, risk assessment, community involvement and post-release monitoring as essential planning elements. Tracking survival and reproduction helps assess results and adapt protection measures.</p><p>Gazelles’ return to the wild therefore depends on continued habitat protection and monitoring of their establishment, alongside the effort invested in releasing them.</p><p class="article-sources"><a href="https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=370557&amp;lang=ar">LANA: fifth batch — 22 November 2026</a> · <a href="https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=357625&amp;lang=ar">LANA: release of 28 gazelles — 17 May 2026</a> · <a href="https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=370395&amp;lang=ar">LANA: release event announcement — 19 November 2026</a> · <a href="https://iucn.org/resources/publication/guidelines-reintroductions-and-other-conservation-translocations">IUCN/SSC: Guidelines for Reintroductions and Other Conservation Translocations, 2013</a></p><p class="photo-credit">Photograph published with the report: Libyan News Agency (LANA). · <a href="https://lana.gov.ly/images/src/370557.jpg">1</a></p>
