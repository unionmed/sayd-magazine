Archive photo.</figcaption></figure><p class="excerpt">Results from the Dubai Racing Carnival opener, with racing returning on 20 November.</p><p>Brotherly Love won the Sheikh Ahmed bin Rashed Al Maktoum Cup, the feature on the Dubai Racing Carnival’s opening night at Meydan on Friday, 6 November 2026. Trained by Britain’s Jamie Osborne and ridden by Danny Tudhope, he beat Sirocco Winds by five and a quarter lengths in the Emirates-sponsored 1,600-metre race.</p><p>In the opening 1,200-metre race, eight-year-old Colour Up recorded his first Meydan victory since 2023. Pat Dobbs rode the Doug Watson-trained horse to a half-length win over Ma Yetal.</p><p>Union Security, trained by Ahmad bin Harmash and ridden by Connor Beasley, won the 1,400-metre Emirates Skywards race for three-year-olds ahead of Magic Art, scoring on opening night for the second consecutive season.</p><p>Saudi trainer Abdullah Al Monif’s two runners, representing AMM Racing and owner Ayman AlRajhi, both finished sixth: Allajayn in a field of six in the Emirates Skywards, and Brave Vekoma in a field of ten in the 1,200-metre Emirates Courier Express maiden.</p><p>Al Monif, 34, began his equestrian career in show jumping before moving into flat racing and gained experience in Ireland, Britain and the United States. He currently trains around 30 horses in Saudi Arabia and hopes his participation in Dubai will give him international experience and test his horses against new rivals.</p><p>The carnival resumes on Friday, 20 November, with the Listed Dubai Creek Mile as the feature. The season comprises 16 race nights running until 12 March 2027.</p><p>Season highlights include Festive Friday on 18 December, Fashion Friday on 22 January and Emirates Super Saturday on 27 February. Following the carnival’s conclusion, Meydan will host the Dubai World Cup on 27 March 2027, featuring nine races with total prize money of US$30.5 million.</p><p>Source: Dubai Racing Club and Emirates News Agency.</p><p class="photo-credit">Photo: Dubai Racing Club</p>
