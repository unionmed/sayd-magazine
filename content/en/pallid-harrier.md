<figure><img src="../../../media/publications/daily-birds-20261106/pallid-harrier.jpg" width="2048" height="1991" alt="Pale grey Pallid Harrier in flight over open ground" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto"><figcaption>Pallid Harrier</figcaption></figure><p class="excerpt">Circus macrourus</p><h2>Identity and identification</h2><p>The Pallid Harrier is a slender Accipitridae raptor. Males are pale grey with narrow black wingtips, while brown females and juveniles resemble other harriers more closely. Head, wing and tail features should be assessed together before identifying brown birds.</p><h2>Habitat and food</h2><p>It hunts low over plains and grassland, taking rodents, small birds, reptiles and insects. The importance of different prey varies between breeding and wintering grounds, rather than remaining uniform everywhere.</p><h2>Breeding and development</h2><p>It nests on the ground among grasses in its Eurasian breeding range. The Peregrine Fund reports 3–6 eggs, incubated by the female for about 29–30 days while the male supplies food. Chicks first fly at about 6–7 weeks and remain with their parents for several more weeks before independence. Winter presence does not imply local nesting.</p><h2>Arab occurrence and seasonality</h2><p>Lebanon’s 2026 guide describes uncommon but regular passage and rare winter presence. Qatar’s list and Oman’s 2026 list instead describe common passage and winter visits. November encounter prospects therefore differ between the Levant and Gulf; Lebanese rarity should not be extended to Arabia.</p><h2>Observation and conservation</h2><p>Its grasslands and fields benefit from vegetation retention and reduced poisoning and disturbance. For an uncertain identification, several views from a safe distance are more useful than chasing a bird for a closer photograph.</p><p class="article-sources"><a href="https://www.avilist.org/checklist/v2026/">AviList Core Team — Global Avian Checklist v2026, 20 October 2026</a> · <a href="https://www.undp.org/lebanon/publications/birds-lebanon-photographic-guide-409-species">UNDP / ABCL / Ministry of Environment — Birds of Lebanon: A Photographic Guide to 409 Species, 18 June 2026</a> · <a href="https://qatarbirds.org/QBList">Qatar Bird Observation Group — Qatar Bird List, consulted 6 November 2026</a> · <a href="https://www.oman-birding.com/sitefiles/78/1/1/781112/Oman_Bird_List_ver_8-0_-_1_Jan_2026-_541_species.xlsx">Oman Bird Records Committee — Oman Bird List v8.0, 1 January 2026</a> · <a href="https://www.birdlife.org.za/red-data-book/red-list/pallid-harrier/">BirdLife South Africa — Pallid Harrier, Regional Red Data Book 2025</a> · <a href="https://peregrinefund.org/explore-raptors-species/hawks/pallid-harrier">The Peregrine Fund — Pallid Harrier: Nest, Eggs and Young</a></p><p class="photo-credit">Species illustration: Sunny Zhang 张迎新 — CC0 1.0. · <a href="https://www.inaturalist.org/photos/383306739">1</a> · <a rel="license" href="https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/">https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/</a></p>
