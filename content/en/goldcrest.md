<figure><img src="../../../media/publications/daily-birds-20261106/goldcrest.jpg" width="1547" height="1547" alt="Small olive-coloured Goldcrest with yellow crown on a branch" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto"><figcaption>Goldcrest</figcaption></figure><p class="excerpt">Regulus regulus</p><h2>Identity and identification</h2><p>The Goldcrest is a tiny Regulidae species with olive upperparts, pale underparts, two pale wing bars and a yellow crown bordered by black. The lack of a bold white eyebrow and dark eye stripe helps distinguish it from Firecrest.</p><h2>Habitat and food</h2><p>It moves actively through branches, especially in conifers, picking off tiny insects. Scan the outer foliage and listen for fine calls without pursuing the bird.</p><h2>Breeding and development</h2><p>BTO reports a typical clutch of 6–8 eggs and female incubation lasting about 16–19 days. Young require care in the nest and normally fledge after 16.5–18 days. British breeding dates should not automatically be transferred to Lebanese woodland.</p><h2>Arab occurrence and seasonality</h2><p>The 2026 Lebanon guide records a scarce, localized resident breeding population alongside very rare, irregular winter visits. November observations may therefore involve local birds or visitors; all Lebanese birds should not be described as winter migrants.</p><h2>Observation and conservation</h2><p>Protecting mixed woodland and conifers from degradation and insecticides preserves its habitat. Photograph birds from paths without approaching or revealing nest sites.</p><p class="article-sources"><a href="https://www.avilist.org/checklist/v2026/">AviList Core Team — Global Avian Checklist v2026, 20 October 2026</a> · <a href="https://www.undp.org/lebanon/publications/birds-lebanon-photographic-guide-409-species">UNDP / ABCL / Ministry of Environment — Birds of Lebanon: A Photographic Guide to 409 Species, 18 June 2026</a> · <a href="https://www.bto.org/learn/about-birds/birdfacts/goldcrest">British Trust for Ornithology — BirdFacts: Goldcrest</a> · <a href="https://www.rspb.org.uk/birds-and-wildlife/goldcrest">RSPB — Goldcrest</a></p><p class="photo-credit">Species illustration: Istvan Sipos — CC0 1.0. · <a href="https://www.inaturalist.org/photos/579201510">1</a> · <a rel="license" href="https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/">https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/</a></p>
