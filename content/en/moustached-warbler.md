<figure><img src="../../../media/publications/daily-birds-20261106/moustached-warbler.jpg" width="1500" height="1000" alt="Moustached Warbler with a broad pale eyebrow clinging to a reed" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto"><figcaption>Moustached Warbler</figcaption></figure><p class="excerpt">Acrocephalus melanopogon</p><h2>Identity and identification</h2><p>The Moustached Warbler is an Acrocephalidae species with a broad pale eyebrow contrasting with a dark crown and darker cheek. It has streaked brown upperparts and pale underparts with brownish flanks; a clear view of the head helps separate similar reedbed warblers.</p><h2>Habitat and food</h2><p>It searches aquatic vegetation and reeds for small invertebrates, including beetles and tiny snails. Dense wetland vegetation is central to its habitat, making drainage and reed burning important pressures.</p><h2>Breeding and development</h2><p>SEO/BirdLife describes a cup nest within aquatic vegetation and a clutch of 3–6 eggs. Both parents incubate for about 14–15 days and feed the chicks, which become capable of flight at around 12 days. Breeding dates vary between regions.</p><h2>Arab occurrence and seasonality</h2><p>The 2026 Lebanon guide lists it as a common summer breeder, scarce passage migrant and rare winter visitor. Breeding abundance does not imply similar numbers in November, when autumn passage overlaps with limited winter presence.</p><h2>Observation and conservation</h2><p>Wait for it at a vegetation edge rather than forcing a route through reeds. Failure to detect this secretive bird during a short visit does not establish absence.</p><p class="article-sources"><a href="https://www.avilist.org/checklist/v2026/">AviList Core Team — Global Avian Checklist v2026, 20 October 2026</a> · <a href="https://www.undp.org/lebanon/publications/birds-lebanon-photographic-guide-409-species">UNDP / ABCL / Ministry of Environment — Birds of Lebanon: A Photographic Guide to 409 Species, 18 June 2026</a> · <a href="https://seo.org/ave/carricerin-real/">SEO/BirdLife — Carricerín real</a></p><p class="photo-credit">Species illustration: egorbirder — CC BY 4.0. · <a href="https://www.inaturalist.org/photos/380926859">1</a> · <a rel="license" href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/">https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/</a></p>
