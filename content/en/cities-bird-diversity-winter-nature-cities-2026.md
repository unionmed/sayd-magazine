A study published in Nature Cities on 19 October 2026 analysed around 17 million eBird records, comparing 452 North American cities with their surrounding areas.</p><p>The team examined species richness rather than simply the number of individual birds, distinguishing native from non-native species and comparing seasons. The north–south difference in native bird diversity was more pronounced in winter, but cities weakened that difference relative to surrounding areas.</p><figure style="max-width:900px;margin:26px auto"><img src="../../../assets/images/2026/10/beirut-aub-pelicans-bassem-20180917-frame60.jpg" alt="Great white pelicans above the American University of Beirut campus, 17 September 2018.
