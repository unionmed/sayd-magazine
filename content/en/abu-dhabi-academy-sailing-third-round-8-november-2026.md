More than 40 male and female sailors from its Abu Dhabi and Al Mirfa programmes are expected to take part, according to Emirates News Agency (WAM).</p><p>The round includes Optimist and ILCA racing, giving young sailors an opportunity to test their skills, from boat handling and reading wind direction to adapting to changing sailing conditions.</p><p>Coaches use these internal rounds to assess participants’ progress and prepare them for domestic and international competition.</p><p>Academy director Khalifa Al Suwaidi said the races support continuous training and the development of new sailors, alongside discipline and a competitive spirit. He also highlighted the academy’s efforts to attract more young participants and provide an environment in which Emirati talent can develop.</p><p>The round will conclude with awards for the leading competitors in the different categories.</p><p class="article-sources"><a href="https://www.wam.ae/ar/article/c34auqz-أكاديمية-أبوظبي-للرياضات-البحرية-تنظم-الجولة">Emirates News Agency (WAM), 6 November 2026</a></p><p class="photo-credit">Photo: Tangopaso / Wikimedia Commons, 2018, public domain.
