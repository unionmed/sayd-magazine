Adults are dark with a white throat and chest. Despite its diving habits, it is not a member of the waterbird family of grebes.</p><p>It forages underwater for small invertebrates in fast streams and moves between rocks close to the water surface. Its habitat depends on the food and shelter provided by a river channel, not simply the presence of water.</p><p>BTO reports a typical clutch of four or five eggs. Sheltered sites beside water, including suitable artificial structures, can provide nest locations. Do not approach nests for photographs.</p><p>Official French accounts extend its range to northwestern African mountains. Maintaining flow, water quality and riverbanks benefits this ecological specialist. The illustrative photograph does not establish occurrence in a particular Arab river; local records require independent documentation.</p><p>BirdLife lists Morocco within its native resident range. This presence remains tied to suitable, patchy habitat and does not mean it is common in every Moroccan or Arab river.</p><p class="article-sources"><a href="https://www.bto.org/learn/about-birds/birdfacts/dipper">www.bto.org — White-throated Dipper</a> · <a href="https://faunesauvagedecorse.oec.fr/Cinclus_cinclus_Linnaeus_1758_mpage_299_idtaxon%2C5190.htm">faunesauvagedecorse.oec.fr — White-throated Dipper</a> · <a href="https://datazone.birdlife.org/species/factsheet/white-throated-dipper-cinclus-cinclus">datazone.birdlife.org — White-throated Dipper</a> · <a href="https://www.mercantour-parcnational.fr/fr/des-connaissances/le-patrimoine-naturel/la-faune-du-mercantour/cincle-plongeur">www.mercantour-parcnational.fr — White-throated Dipper</a></p><p class="photo-credit">Photo: Dario Taraborelli</p>
