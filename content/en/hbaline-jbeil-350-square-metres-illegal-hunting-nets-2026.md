<figure><img src="../../../media/publications/hbaline-jbeil-nets-20261117/1002708285.jpg" width="4000" height="1800" alt="Anti-poaching team and Internal Security Forces personnel during the net-removal operation in Hbaline, Jbeil" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto"></figure><p class="excerpt">A field operation following a report by sustainable hunters, in cooperation with the Internal Security Forces’ Jbeil Police Station.</p><p>The Anti-Poaching Unit (APU) removed 350 square metres of illegal hunting nets set on a hill overlooking a valley in Hbaline, in the Jbeil district. The operation was conducted in cooperation and coordination with the Internal Security Forces’ Jbeil Police Station.</p><figure><img src="../../../media/publications/hbaline-jbeil-nets-20261117/1002708259.jpg" width="2592" height="1168" alt="Hunting nets strung between trees on the hillside in Hbaline" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto"></figure><p>The team acted on a report from sustainable hunters in the Jbeil area, according to the Middle East Center for Sustainable Harvest and Anti-Poaching (MECSHAP). The joint Lebanese–international operations structure of MECSHAP and CABS carried out the mission through its field Anti-Poaching Unit.</p><figure><img src="../../../media/publications/hbaline-jbeil-nets-20261117/1002708264.jpg" width="1168" height="2592" alt="Field team beside hunting nets in Hbaline" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto"></figure><p>The centre said the nets are used for indiscriminate, non-selective hunting and threaten birds and wildlife by trapping different bird species without distinction, including protected species.</p><figure><img src="../../../media/publications/hbaline-jbeil-nets-20261117/1002708292.jpg" width="4000" height="1800" alt="An Internal Security Forces officer carrying a bundle of removed hunting nets" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto"></figure><p>It added that Jbeil Police Station is continuing its investigation to identify the person responsible for installing the nets, with a view to taking the necessary legal action.</p><figure><img src="../../../media/publications/hbaline-jbeil-nets-20261117/1002708312.jpg" width="4000" height="1800" alt="Collected hunting nets and equipment on a vehicle after the Hbaline operation" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto"></figure><p class="photo-credit">Photo source: MECSHAP – 16 November 2026.</p>
