<figure><img src="../../../media/publications/mecshap-berri-hunting-fines-20261123/mecshap-berri-meeting.jpg" width="1536" height="1517" alt="A photograph from the meeting between a MECSHAP delegation and Parliament Speaker Nabih Berri." loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto"></figure><p class="excerpt">Adonis El Khatib and Shireen Bou Rafoul present the Anti-Poaching Unit’s activities and its cooperation with security and judicial authorities.</p><p>Adonis El Khatib, President of the Middle East Center for Sustainable Harvest and Anti-Poaching (MECSHAP) and Editor-in-Chief of Sayd Magazine, visited Lebanese Parliament Speaker Nabih Berri. He was accompanied by Shireen Bou Rafoul, a member of the centre’s governing board and the representative in Lebanon of the Committee Against Bird Slaughter (CABS).</p><p>The delegation briefed Berri on the main activities and field operations carried out by the centre’s Anti-Poaching Unit (APU), in cooperation with CABS and in coordination with security and judicial authorities and the relevant official bodies.</p><p>The meeting addressed the importance of strengthening legislation and deterrent measures, particularly the need to increase fines for hunting violations in proportion to the seriousness of offences against wildlife. The aim is to curb these practices and protect Lebanon’s natural heritage.</p><p>Berri praised the efforts of the centre and CABS, expressing appreciation for their initiatives and for the sense of responsibility shown by those involved in serving nature and society in cooperation with state institutions.</p><p class="photo-credit">Photo: MECSHAP.</p>
